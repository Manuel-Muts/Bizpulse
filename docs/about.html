About BizPulse

BizPulse, founded in 2026 by MUTSTECH LTD, is dedicated to transforming how small and medium businesses manage their operations. Our mission is to empower entrepreneurs with intuitive tools that provide real-time insights into inventory, streamline order processing, and offer a clear overview of financial health. We believe that every business, regardless of size, deserves access to world-class management solutions.

Our vision is led by CEO Emmanuel Mutegi, who champions innovation and customer-centric design. At BizPulse, we are committed to continuous improvement, ensuring our platform evolves with your business needs, helping you make smarter decisions and achieve sustainable growth.
